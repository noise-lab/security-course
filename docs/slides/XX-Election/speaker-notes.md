# Bonus — Speaker Notes (Election Security)

Per-slide context + clickable links. Bonus deck (off-schedule). In-deck notes (`::: {.notes}`) are visible via reveal.js speaker view (press **S**). Terse — enough for a future instructor to pick up.

| Slide | Context + Links |
|---|---|
| **Why Elections Are a Security Problem** | Anchor: [Stark & Wagner, "Evidence-Based Elections" (IEEE S&P 2012)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2003174) — quote is from the abstract. Security here means *public evidence* that convinces even the losing side. |
| **Getting It Right vs. Proving It** | 1948 "Dewey Defeats Truman" as the archetype of a confident result with no evidence behind it. Modern parallels: FL 2000 (butterfly ballot / hanging chads); WI/MI/PA 2016 recount attempts that were largely stymied because the systems couldn't produce evidence. |
| **The Core Requirements** | Walk the list. Emphasize the conflicts: privacy vs. auditability (secret ballot is receipt-free by design); accessibility vs. transparency (accessible interfaces are opaque to observers). Anchor: [NASEM, *Securing the Vote* (2018)](https://www.nap.edu/catalog/25120/securing-the-vote-protecting-american-democracy) — the most cited modern consensus report. |
| **Where Accuracy and Availability Fail** | Long lines = availability failure = security failure. Lever-machine "jam at 99" is a classic silent-accuracy anecdote worth telling. |
| **A Century of Voting Technology** | [Help America Vote Act (2002)](https://www.eac.gov/about/help-america-vote-act) — ~$3B triggered by FL 2000. Most states bought paperless DRE touchscreens — trading one problem for a worse one. |
| **The DRE Detour** | DRE = the vote exists only as bits the machine chose to write. Sets up software independence and the Diebold attack. |
| **Threat Model: No One Is Trusted** | Design assumption: all parties suspect. Motivates software independence. |
| **The Diebold Study: Stealing Votes in Software** | [Feldman, Halderman & Felten, "Security Analysis of the Diebold AccuVote-TS Voting Machine" (2006/2007)](https://citp.princeton.edu/our-work/voting/) and the [demonstration video](https://citp.princeton.edu/our-work/voting/). Canonical "voting machine is just a PC" result. Physical access to one machine + shared memory cards = a county. |
| **Software Independence** | [Rivest & Wack, "On the notion of 'software independence' in voting systems" (2006)](https://people.csail.mit.edu/rivest/pubs/RW06.pdf). The phrase to remember: "software is not to be trusted." Paper is the only artifact an attacker who owns the software cannot silently rewrite. |
| **Certification Doesn't Save You** | Federal: [EAC VVSG](https://www.eac.gov/voting-equipment/voluntary-voting-system-guidelines). ITAs paid by vendors — conflict of interest. Certification checks *works as specified*, not *secure against an adversary*. |
| **The Modern Stack** | BMDs: the accessibility/paper compromise. Open research question: do voters actually verify the printout? Anchor: [Bernhard et al., "Can Voters Detect Malicious Manipulation of Ballot-Marking Devices?" (S&P 2020)](https://jhalderm.com/pub/papers/bmd-verifiability-sp20.pdf) — most don't. Appel & Stark critique of BMDs: [Andrew Appel's writing on BMDs](https://freedom-to-tinker.com/2020/10/06/ballot-marking-devices/). |
| **The Paper Trail Across the Country** | [Verified Voting's Verifier map](https://verifiedvoting.org/verifier/). **2024 vignette:** ~98% of 2024 votes on paper-based systems (Verified Voting / Brennan Center). Paperless-DRE era nearly over. |
| **Risk-Limiting Audits** | Anchor: [Lindeman & Stark, "A Gentle Introduction to Risk-Limiting Audits" (S&P Magazine 2012)](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~stark/Preprints/gentle12.pdf). Vora's "brush your teeth; eat your spinach; audit your elections!" line. |
| **RLAs in Practice, 2024–2025** | **Verify before class.** **Georgia 2024 statewide RLA**, all 159 counties, 5% risk limit: [GA Secretary of State risk-limiting audit page](https://sos.ga.gov/page/risk-limiting-audit-post-election-audit). **Virginia RLAs (2024, 2025)**: [Virginia Dept. of Elections](https://www.elections.virginia.gov/casting-a-ballot/election-security/risk-limiting-audits/). RLA adoption tracking: [Verified Voting Audit Law Database](https://verifiedvoting.org/auditlaw/) and [Brennan Center on RLAs](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/risk-limiting-audits). |
| **Disinformation: Attacking Belief, Not Ballots** | **NH January 2024 Biden deepfake robocall**: [FCC Order banning AI-voiced robocalls under TCPA (Feb 2024)](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-makes-ai-generated-voices-robocalls-illegal); the perpetrator (political consultant Steve Kramer) faced [charges and a proposed $6M FCC fine](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-proposes-6m-fine-illegal-robocalls-made-ai-voice-cloning-tech). Post-2024 assessment: [Harvard Ash Center / Kennedy School analyses](https://ash.harvard.edu/) — biggest 2024 misinformation narratives (pet-eating, FEMA) were *not* AI-driven; cheap-fakes and human rumor dominated. |
| **Why "Blockchain Voting" Is Not the Answer** | Anchor: [Park, Specter, Narula, Rivest, "Going from bad to worse: from Internet voting to blockchain voting" (JCS 2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13272). Blockchain authenticates transactions on a ledger, not humans casting secret ballots. E2E-verifiable schemes ([STAR-Vote](https://www.usenix.org/conference/evtwote13/workshop-program/presentation/bell), [ElectionGuard](https://www.electionguard.vote/)) are the serious research direction. |
| **What to Remember** | Land arc: paper + RLAs largely solved the *machine* trust problem this decade; open problem is sustaining *public trust* against cheap, scalable disinformation. No direct debate cross-link — this bonus deck is standalone. |
